How to Use This Tool

1. Switch Worlds

Use the top menu switcher to jump between Earth, Mars, and the Moon.

2. Custom Locations

When viewing Mars or the Moon, use the right sidebar panel to input custom latitude, longitude, and location names. North and East values are positive whereas the South and West values are negative.

3. Find Earth Matches

Run the algorithm on your saved custom locations to pinpoint analog coordinates and error radii on Earth.

About Celestial Parallels

We developed a scientifically rigorous Earth analog site selection interactive map integrated with 3D CesiumJS/ion. Our model addresses the issue of there being no rigorous, efficient way to find earth analogs of lunar and martian surfaces. It uses widespread open source planetary data and enforces an area-weighted sub aerial land mask to exclude marine environments, This approach achieves sub-10 km spatial precision while filtering out water, urban, and high-vegetation anomalies. All the user needs to do is input their desired location, and our algorithm will display the closest earth analog based on various parameters such as topology, geology, water content, wind and so on and so forth. This allows for much more rapid location of suitable analogs, allows greater optimization of rover transport on the moon and mars, and it dramatically improves the experience and readiness of biological personnel for extraterrestrial conditions. Our project bridges heavy spatial data science with a 3D digital twin to accurately map Martian and lunar location to their true terrestrial equivalents.
