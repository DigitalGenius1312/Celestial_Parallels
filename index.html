How to Use This Tool

1. Switch & Navigate Worlds

Use the top menu switcher to jump between Earth, Mars, and the Moon. On Earth, hold down the middle mouse button (scroll wheel) and drag to rotate along the Z-axis, allowing you to tilt the camera into a traditional ground-level perspective. On Mars or the Moon, double-click or right-click anywhere on the 3D globe to auto-fill coordinates.

2. Custom Locations & Weights

When viewing Mars or the Moon, use the right sidebar panel to select from default preset locations of famous landmarks and bases, or input custom latitude, longitude, and location names (North and East are positive; South and West are negative). Click the Weights button in the panel header to adjust category importance or apply mission profiles like Rover Mobility and ISRU Drilling.

3. Find, Compare & Export Matches

Run the algorithm on your selected locations to pinpoint analog coordinates, percentage match, and error radii on Earth. Hover over or click any C1–C5 parameter bar to inspect individual sub-parameter scores, open the Raw Parameter Comparison table to verify physical measurements, or export your findings as GIS CSVs and formatted Mission Brief PDFs.

About Celestial Parallels

We developed a scientifically rigorous Earth analog site selection interactive map integrated with 3D CesiumJS/ion. Our model addresses the lack of an efficient, rigorous method to identify terrestrial analogs of lunar and Martian surfaces.

It utilizes widespread open-source planetary data and enforces an area-weighted subaerial land mask to exclude marine environments. This approach achieves sub-10 km spatial precision while filtering out water, urban, and high-vegetation anomalies.

Once a user selects or inputs a target location, the algorithm identifies the closest Earth analog across five weighted parameter groups—Mineralogy (C1), Topography (C2), Thermal Properties (C3), Subsurface Resources (C4), and Eolian Dynamics (C5)—enabling rapid analog site selection, rover transport optimization, and improved biological crew readiness for extraterrestrial conditions.
