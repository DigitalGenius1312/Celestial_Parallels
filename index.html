How to Use This Explorer

1. Switch Worlds

Use the top-left switcher to jump between Earth, Mars, and the Moon. Each body loads real 3D terrain and planetary proportions.

2. Navigate in 3D

  • Rotate: Left-click and drag
  • Zoom: Scroll wheel up/down
  • Tilt / Orbit: Hold middle-click (or Ctrl + drag)

3. Fly to Landmarks

Click any location pill in the top-right panel to smoothly glide down from orbit directly to famous craters, mountains, and landing sites.

About Solar System Explorer

This interactive 3D web application lets you explore planetary surfaces across our inner solar system directly in your browser without installing heavy software.

Powered By:

  • CesiumJS: Open-source 3D geospatial globe engine
  • Planetary Ellipsoids: Accurate WGS84 (Earth), Mars, and Lunar reference spheres
  • 3D Tilesets: High-resolution elevation and imagery streamed via Cesium ion
